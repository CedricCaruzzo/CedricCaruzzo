# Cédric Caruzzo

AI Research Scientist at Lunit working on reliable foundation models for biomedical AI.

My research studies how learned systems generalize, adapt, and fail when their data or evidence changes. I work across representation learning, LLM post-training, retrieval, and evaluation.

## Selected research

### Deceptive Grounding

Clinical RAG can faithfully cite real evidence while attributing it to the wrong medical entity. We characterized this failure across 13 models and a deployed system, then proposed entity-attribution verification.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09349) · [Project page](https://cedriccaruzzo.github.io/blog/deceptive_grounding.html)

### CellPainTR

A transformer for cross-dataset Cell Painting representations that generalizes to unseen datasets without fine-tuning.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06986) · [Code](https://github.com/CellPainTR/CellPainTR) · [Project page](https://cedriccaruzzo.github.io/blog/cellpaintr.html)
